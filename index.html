How to answer the questions with blanks

Complete the question first, then choose its answer. The yellow blanks are missing parts of the question. The final answer is still one option: A, B, C or D.

Before starting

Have the lecturer-issued CHE 111 Test 1 (Stream 2) Word List Sheet printed and beside you, together with your working paper and any expressly permitted calculator. Write your name and student number on the sheet. If you cannot access a printed copy, arrange an approved alternative with your lecturer before starting. The question timer continues while you consult a digital word list.

For each question

  1. Note the original question number on the slide. Find that same question number on the Word List Sheet, even if questions appear in a shuffled order.
  2. Read the yellow blanks labelled (a), (b), (c) and so on. Under the matching question number on your Word List Sheet, the missing words, numbers or formulae are jumbled and numbered 1, 2, 3 and so on.
  3. Match each blank to the correct numbered entry. Each listed entry is used once, within its own question only. Note your matches on your working paper. Lowercase blank labels are different from the uppercase answer options.
  4. Read the completed question, do any required calculation, and choose one answer: A, B, C or D. Keep your final choice beside the original question number on your working paper.
  5. Transfer your final A–D choices to the corresponding original question numbers in the authorised answer grid. The numbered word-list matches are preparation for answering; do not enter a word-list number as your A–D answer. Follow any additional submission directions given by your lecturer.

Practice example (not a test answer): “A ___ (a) measures ___ (b).” Word list: 1. temperature; 2. thermometer. Match (a) = 2 and (b) = 1, then read “A thermometer measures temperature.” In the test, use the completed question to decide which A–D option is correct.

The yellow blanks are part of an image: you cannot type into them on this page. Use your word list and working paper.

Viewing, timing and assessment rules

  1. Work independently. The issued Word List Sheet is an authorised assessment resource. Do not use AI, messaging, unauthorised devices, websites or outside assistance. Use only other materials and calculators expressly permitted by your lecturer.
  2. Charge your device, check your connection and enable Do Not Disturb. Make sure you can read the questions comfortably.
  3. Questions stay visible for their full allocated question time. The next question appears automatically when that time expires.
  4. The timer keeps running while away and after refresh. Expired questions cannot be reopened. Resume using the same browser and student number.
  5. Use + Larger or − Smaller to adjust question size. Scroll across an enlarged question to read all of it. Use Reset size to restore the default.
  6. Do not copy, photograph or share questions.
  7. Use the designated answer-grid button when it appears.. Enter your final A, B, C or D choice against the original question number shown, even if the order is shuffled.
  8. When the TIME UP/submission screen appears, submit your recorded A–D answers through the authorised answer grid or the method specified by your lecturer, before the submission deadline. Do not treat the TIME UP card as another question. Closing this viewer does not submit answers. No incident report is automatically sent to your lecturer.
